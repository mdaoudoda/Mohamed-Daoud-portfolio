Academic Journey

Driven by a passion for electronics and engineering systems, I chose an academic path to learn how electronics work, how actuators and electrical machines are designed and controlled, and how to connect everything together into a system while adapting new technologies.

From Personal Interest to Academic Expertise

My love for traveling, cars, and aircraft inspired a deep interest in speed and high-torque machines, creating a desire to learn how to build, maintain, repair, and develop them. This initial curiosity shaped my education and guided my career into research and development, where I completed my Master's degree working on renewable energy and Vehicle Powertrain systems.

Career Motivation & Focus

Because I always found myself working at the intersection of software development and hardware design, transitioning into systems engineering was a natural next step. This unique balance has allowed me to seamlessly manage complex projects and bridge the gap between physical hardware and digital control systems throughout my career.

Career Insights

6+ years of experience in R&D and system engineering across different metiers : System desing and simulation, Control, power electronics and functional safety.
