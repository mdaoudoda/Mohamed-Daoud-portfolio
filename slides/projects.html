Aerospace, Automotive & Powertrains

Aerospace Linear Actuator & Magnetic Latching Design for Inerting Valves Goal: Design and optimize a compact (<1 kg), high-temperature (200°C) linear actuator and magnetic latching system delivering uniform force over a 6 mm stroke for an aircraft fuel-tank inerting shut-off valve. Tools: JMAG-Designer (2D/3D FEA), MATLAB (SQP / fmincon optimization), Design of Experiments (DOE), magnetic permeance network modeling. Scope: Selected SmCo permanent magnets and high-saturation soft magnetic alloys for 200°C operation; combined JMAG DOE sensitivity modeling with MATLAB SQP optimization to size a 35 N low-mass magnetic latch, developed a permeance-based radial moving-magnet actuator validated via adaptive-mesh FEA, and sized a thermally constrained 1000-turn copper coil (25 V / 1 A).
Valeo iBSG 48V Belt Starter Generator(IBSG) — Platform / HMC Goal: Ensure ASPICE-compliant system architecture, functional fault management, and verification traceability for a 48V mild-hybrid Belt Starter Generator platform. Tools: A-SPICE, ISO 26262, SysML, Enterprise Architect, IBM DOORS, MATLAB/Simulink, Stateflow, dSPACE. Scope: Defined internal system interface specifications, start-up sequences, and On-Board Diagnostics (OBD) requirements; maintained the Fault State Matrix (FSM), refined system requirement specifications, and analyzed system test case results to isolate and resolve hardware/software integration issues.
800V SiC High-Voltage Traction Inverter (RSA Flex Evan) Goal: Architect and specify safety-critical functional and system requirements (SYS.1–SYS.3) for a next-generation 800V Silicon Carbide (SiC) electric vehicle traction inverter. Tools: A-SPICE (SYS.1–SYS.3), ISO 26262 (ASIL), SysML/UML, Siemens Polarion, Enterprise Architect, MATLAB/Simulink, Stateflow. Scope: Elicited and authored system architecture and functional specifications covering operating state machines, safe-state management, AC voltage estimation, DC-link thermal derating, electromechanical power conversion, Electromagnetic Compatibility (EMC), and lifetime endurance.
EV Powertrain Benchmarking (Tazzari Zero vs. Renault Twizy) Goal: Model, control, and benchmark the energy efficiency, battery autonomy, and dynamic traction performance of the Tazzari Zero and Renault Twizy electric vehicles. Tools: MATLAB/Simulink, Energetic Macroscopic Representation (EMR). Scope: Built full EMR powertrain models covering battery dynamics, power converter, DC machines, mechanical transmissions, and road-load physics; synthesized closed-loop current and duty-cycle controllers for traction and regenerative braking, and simulated 1-passenger, 10° incline, and 1-hour range scenarios to evaluate battery-vs-torque design trade-offs.
Electromagnetic Design, FEA Optimization & Self-Controled PMSM Goal: Execute end-to-end analytical sizing, 2D finite element optimization, and closed-loop power electronic drive control for a 1.5 kVA single-phase transformer and a 1.5 kW Brushless Permanent Magnet Synchronous Machine (PMSM). Tools: MATLAB, FEMM (2D Finite Element Analysis), Lua scripting, PSIM, PWM & PI pole-placement control. Scope: Minimized transformer volume via MATLAB geometric sweeps and extracted equivalent circuit impedances in FEMM; sized an 18-slot NdFeB PMSM, automated dynamic rotor stepping via Lua scripts, and reshaped stator slots (Evershed criterion) to eliminate cogging torque and EMF harmonics; simulated PWM choppers and three-phase inverters in PSIM with PI current loops and 30° rotor-synchronized self-control (autopilotage).
VAL Railway System & Energy Simulation for Efficiency Inspection Goal: Evaluate powertrain component efficiency and maximize regenerative braking energy recovery for a Lille Metro VAL vehicle operating on a fixed 2-minute station headway. Tools: MATLAB/Simulink. Scope: Modeled a single VAL vehicle powertrain operating in both motor and generator modes coupled to Lille's fixed-voltage DC grid; inspected energy losses across the traction machine, inverter, and gearbox while simulating varied speed profiles to optimize regenerative braking output under a 2-minute vehicle-to-vehicle frequency constraint.

Renewable Energy & Microgrids

Passive House PV, Battery & Inverter Sizing Goal: Optimize overall system cost and minimize initial CAPEX (Capital Expenditure) by sizing a modular residential solar-plus-storage architecture capable of scaling over time. Tools: Matlab-Simulink optimization toolboxes. Scope: Developed an offline sizing optimization model utilizing 1-year historical load consumption, fixed grid electricity tariffs, and 1-year PV generation profiles to determine optimal PV array capacity, battery pack size, and inverter power rating.
Design of Power Electronic Control drive of a DFIG used in Large Turbines Goal: Maximize wind energy extraction and ensure unity power factor grid integration for a 2 MW Doubly Fed Induction Generator (DFIG) wind turbine across sub-synchronous and hypersynchronous operating modes. Tools: MATLAB/Simulink (Simscape), PSIM. Scope: Modeled wind turbine aerodynamics, MPPT algorithms, and a bidirectional Back-to-Back converter (RSC/GSC) with DC-link and grid filters; analyzed fault protection schemes (crowbar, series resistor, DC-chopper) and validated dynamic performance under wind speed and reactive power steps.
PV Solar Panel Modelling (SDM/DDM) & Irradiation Modelling Goal: Construct single and double-diode solar panels models for comparative yield assessment. Tools: NumPy, SciPy, MATLAB-simulink. Scope: comparaison of market available solar panels datsheets and construct electrical and thermal models using SDM/DDM approaches , fit curve the models parameters using optimization techniques and couple the model with MPPT algorithms and real time weather forecast API and static irradiation models for different scenarios.
EMS & Hybrid Inverter Modelling for BESS Goal: Build an intelligent grid-battery storage management loop. Tools: Matlab-simulink, Python, CAN bus protocols. Scope : Engineered high-level EMS arbitration state machines that handle VPP commands, local Grid price, battery SOC, Solar generation and vaious events such as islanding/ voltage & frequency drops while preserving battery health rules.
Sonnen Battery Device Modeling & Efficiency Inspection Goal: Inspect thermal degradation profiles and round-trip efficiency of standard residential storage units. Tools: Matlab/Simulink, Python data wrappers. Scope: Isolated battery electrical, BMS and thermal model on cell and module and pack levels to simulate and analyze round-trip energy efficency for different charge/discharge profiles.
