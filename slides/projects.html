Selected Projects

Aerospace, Automotive & Mass Transit Powertrains

Aircraft Cargo Inerting System Electrical Valve Design Goal: Develop a reliable valve actuator for gas inerting functions. Tools: Ansys Workbench, MATLAB/Simulink. Scope Solved: Met DO-160 flight weight limits while maintaining high seat seating profiles during severe bus voltage drops.
Valeo iBSG 48V Belt Starter Generator Goal: Perform system optimization and signal mapping for a mild-hybrid setup. Tools: MATLAB/Simulink, SabreRD, Vector tools. Scope Solved: Integrated low-voltage transient stability loops that smoothly handle high-current draws during cold-cranks.
Valeo 800V SiC Inverter & 4x1 Powertrain Goal: Simulate high-density Silicon Carbide traction inverters. Tools: PLECS, SabreRD, MATLAB. Scope Solved: Modeled ultra-fast switching profiles to solve high dV/dt concerns while significantly cutting total switching losses.
Tazzari, Renault Twizy & Zoe Powertrain Simulation (MBD/HIL) Goal: Conduct validation and HIL powertrain testing for micro-EVs. Tools: dSPACE HIL, CANoe, CANape, Simscape. Scope Solved: Executed high-to-low voltage vehicle plant dynamics to completely validate battery state-of-charge calculation loops.
PMSM Sizing with Embedded Inverter & Battery Goal: Size a synchronous motor with integrated inverter packages. Tools: Ansys Maxwell, Motor-CAD, Simulink. Scope Solved: Solved dense geometric thermal bounds between the active machine coils and the embedded housing to maintain smooth transient torque profiles.
VAL Railway System & Energy Simulation for Efficiency Inspection Goal: Simulate mass transit network efficiency. Tools: Custom multi-domain MATLAB frameworks. Scope Solved: Isolated grid network losses by tracking vehicle-to-vehicle power transfers via overlapping regenerative braking cycles.

Renewable Energy & Microgrids

BSE Passive House PV, Battery & Inverter Sizing Goal: Balance residential generation and storage capacities based on forecasting data. Tools: Python, PVsyst, Simulink optimization toolboxes. Scope Solved: Authored a Balance of System (BOS) algorithm that maps weather inputs directly to usage arrays to boost passive house autonomy.
Wind Turbine System Control & Conversion Goal: Optimize plant power curves under highly volatile weather fronts. Tools: FAST, state-space toolboxes, Simulink. Scope Solved: Designed closed-loop active pitch control laws that maximize tracking during low winds but reduce mechanical fatigue during high shears.
PV Solar Panel Modelling (SDM/DDM) & Irradiation Approaches Goal: Construct single and double-diode solar panels models for comparative yield assessment. Tools: NumPy, SciPy, MATLAB parameter estimators. Scope Solved: Resolved mathematical solver convergence issues under variable atmospheric fronts by finding stable diode ideality boundaries.
EMS & Hybrid Inverter Modelling for BESS Goal: Build an intelligent grid storage infrastructure management loop. Tools: Stateflow, Python, Modbus protocols. Scope Solved: Engineered high-level EMS arbitration state machines that handle sudden grid peak-shaving needs while preserving battery health rules.
Sonnen Battery System Modeling & Efficiency Inspection Goal: Inspect thermal degradation profiles and round-trip efficiency of standard residential storage units. Tools: Python data wrappers, battery aging libraries. Scope Solved: Isolated internal resistance trends that flagged transient bottlenecks, rearranging software safety triggers to optimize round-trip energy returns.

Core Simulation Frameworks & Mathematics

SIMHUB Simulation Tool Using FMI Framework Goal: Set up a multi-rate co-simulation orchestrator. Tools: C++, Modelica/Dymola, FMI standard APIs. Scope Solved: Formulated functional integration wrappers that synchronize models of vastly different time steps (fast electronics vs. slow mechanical bodies) with no master scheduling deadlocks.
Optimization Solver Application & Adaptation Goal: Refine and deploy non-linear solver architectures to handle heavily bounded layouts. Tools: Python (SciPy Optimization, Pyomo), C++. Scope Solved: Created constraint relaxation parameters over non-linear engineering models, speeding up solver iteration rates for real-time applications.
Switched Reluctance Motor Rotor Geometry Optimization Goal: Minimize native operational acoustic noise signature and structural ripples. Tools: Ansys Maxwell (FEA), Genetic optimization solvers. Scope Solved: Executed high-volume parametric pole-skewing sweeps to identify geometry thresholds that successfully limit ripples without shrinking motor efficiency.
